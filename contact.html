Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about milk deliveries, chilling standards, and billing.

What time does the milk arrive at my doorstep?
Our refrigerated delivery vans complete neighborhood routes between 04:30 AM and 07:00 AM daily. We place your fresh bottles directly inside your insulated doorstep cooler box.
Can I pause my subscription when going on vacation?
Yes! You can pause or resume deliveries instantly via your Farm Dashboard or by sending a quick WhatsApp message to our dispatch desk up to 8:00 PM the evening prior.
How do returnable glass bottle deposits work?
