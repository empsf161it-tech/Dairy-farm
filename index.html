Our Pastoral Heritage

Rooted in Pasture Care, Gentle Milking & Ecological Harmony.

Founded in 1982 in the sunny Green Meadow basin, Aura Dairy Farm was built on a foundational promise: cows living under the open sky yield milk of incomparable sweetness and vital nourishment.

Our herd roams across 640 acres of rotational organic pastures free of chemical fertilizers, pesticides, or GMOs. We practice humane calf-at-foot milking, continuous holistic grazing, and state-of-the-art cold-chain sanitation.
